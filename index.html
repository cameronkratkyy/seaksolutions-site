Questions

I'm not a property manager. Is this for me?

Yes, if your office repeats a paperwork process every week: client intake, collecting documents, signed approvals, follow-ups. Owner onboarding is just my most detailed example.

Can you work in our software?

AppFolio, Buildium, Google, your e-sign tool, or whatever you use: I check your plan, user roles, and features in the free fit check. I won't promise a connection I haven't checked.

How do you keep our accounts and data secure?

You give me a named user with least-privilege access, plus multi-factor sign-in where your software offers it. I back up your current setup before any change. At the end of the project, automations move to your accounts, my access and API tokens are revoked, and your data is returned or deleted within 30 days of the fix window closing. Suspected unauthorized access is reported within 24 hours of discovery.

Are you using AI? Does our data go into it?

Yes. An AI coding assistant helps me build, and I review and test its work before delivery. Your live records aren't sent to any AI model without your written OK and a data plan, and your data is never used to train AI models.

What do your document “checks” mean?

The setup collects the documents, flags what's missing or expired, and sends exceptions to your reviewer. I don't certify insurance, tax, or license status.
