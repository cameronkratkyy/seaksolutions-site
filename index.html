Questions

Usually. I check on the free call and won't promise a connection I haven't checked.

Are you using AI? How is our data protected?

Yes. An AI coding assistant helps me build, and I review and test its output before delivery. Here's how I protect your data: you give me a named user with least-privilege access and multi-factor authentication (MFA) where your software supports it, and I never ask for or hold your passwords. I develop and test on synthetic data first. Your live records are never sent to an AI model without your written OK, and never used for model training. At the end of the project, my access and API tokens are revoked, and your data is returned or deleted within 30 days of the fix window closing.
